# NetLet Marketplace — Design Direction

## Three initial approaches

### 1. Sunlit Souk
**Very Brief Intro:** A refined contemporary marketplace that borrows the clarity of a modern department store and the warmth of regional commerce. Ivory surfaces, a confident apricot accent, and editorial product imagery make discovery feel considered rather than crowded.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Midnight Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, high-contrast marketplace with metallic details and dense information bands. It would feel efficient, urban, and optimized for frequent power shoppers.

**Probability:** 0.03

### 3. Paper & Parcel
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile catalogue-style storefront inspired by printed mail-order books and shipping labels. Structured columns, ink-like typography, and playful labels would make the experience feel personable and collectible.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach: Sunlit Souk

### Design Movement
**Contemporary regional editorial retail.** Soukora combines the disciplined hierarchy of premium department-store ecommerce with a Mediterranean/Middle Eastern daylight sensibility. It is a commerce interface with room to breathe, not a crowded digital bazaar.

### Core Principles
1. **Confident selection:** Clearly prioritise seasonal products, useful categories, and buyer confidence over excessive promotional noise.
2. **Warm precision:** Pair airy ivory surfaces with exact alignment, crisp typography, and concise utility copy.
3. **Tactile discovery:** Use clipped paper-like cards, soft shadows, and sunlit product photography to make browsing feel physical.
4. **Visible utility:** Delivery, search, save, and cart actions remain immediately recognisable at every viewport.

### Color Philosophy
The canvas is a soft **pearl white**, chosen to feel clear and premium without the clinical glare of pure white. **NetLet Orange** carries brand energy in primary buttons and active states, while a **luxurious deep navy** establishes confident contrast for navigation, pricing, and editorial surfaces. Pale citrus-yellow accents appear sparingly as promotional markers and signal optimism without visual noise.

### Layout Paradigm
The home page follows a **retail promenade** rather than a centered landing-page stack. A narrow utility strip anchors the top, product departments form a horizontal wayfinding rail, and the hero operates as an offset editorial stage—copy on the left, visual commerce on the right. Subsequent content alternates between full-bleed deal bands, horizontally scrolling category capsules, and asymmetric product collections.

### Signature Elements
- A **sun-arc brand mark** that resembles both a market awning and a rising horizon.
- **Stamped offer labels** in warm yellow with compact black lettering.
- Delicate **apricot contour lines** and paper-grain texture on large neutral surfaces.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel direct and reassuring. Product cards lift subtly on hover, buttons compress slightly on press, and shopping actions give discreet confirmation through a toast—not an interruptive overlay. Navigation remains instant and legible; discovery surfaces can glide, but never perform theatrically.

### Animation
Use 160–220ms custom ease-out transitions for hover and selection. The hero visual and collection cards may enter with 20–40px upward movement plus opacity, staggered in 50ms steps. Carousels should have controlled inertia and clear affordances. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid looping decorative animation.

### Typography System
**Plus Jakarta Sans** supplies clean, highly legible interface copy and product information. **DM Serif Display** is reserved for large campaign headlines and selected editorial headings, creating a measured contrast between commerce utility and aspiration. Headlines use bold sentence case, categories use medium-weight compact labels, and prices use strong tabular numerals where available.

### Brand Essence
**NetLet is the sunlit marketplace for Kuwait and the Gulf, bringing the reassurance of one trusted storefront to everyday finds and considered upgrades.**

Personality: **warm, discerning, dependable.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, sensory, and specific; CTAs are verbs that point to a clear outcome; microcopy anticipates practical buyer questions without jargon.

> “Small upgrades. A brighter everyday.”

> “Delivered to your door, without the detour.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark uses a gently rounded, weighted sans word shape with a cut-out sun arc above the final “a.” The accompanying symbol is a bold, text-free **apricot awning/sunrise monogram** formed from three nested arches. It must remain recognizable at favicon size and sit visibly in the header.

### Signature Brand Color
**NetLet Orange — #F2683A.**

## Style Decisions

- Sellable product cards use tangible, sunlit product photography rather than illustrative placeholders; symbolic graphics remain limited to category wayfinding.
- Kuwait-specific reassurance is expressed through delivery timing, familiar payment context, and understated neighbourhood references, never decorative tourism clichés.
- NetLet’s supplied roof-mark logo, warm-yellow offer stamps, and apricot contour lines recur across hero, merchandising, and buyer-confidence bands.
